Common questions

A few things to know.

Is the interactive preview a real AI chatbot?

No. It uses prewritten sample responses to demonstrate the idea. A live assistant can be installed separately using a platform such as Tidio.

Can an assistant confirm appointments?

Only when a booking integration has been configured and tested. Otherwise, it can collect an appointment request for your team to confirm.

Will AI replace my staff?

The goal is to help your team handle repetitive tasks. People still handle exceptions, decisions, and customer relationships.

Are the software tools free forever?

Some tools offer free plans or trials. AI conversations, integrations, and other features may require paid subscriptions as usage grows.

Can you guarantee more sales?

No. Results depend on your traffic, offer, workflow, and how the system is used. Start with a measurable task and review the results.
